Payments. Paying online by bank card

Our website is connected to internet acquiring, so you can pay for the Service with a Visa, Mastercard or UnionPay bank card. Once you confirm the selected Product or service, a secure window will open with the payment page of ForteBank JSC, where you need to enter your bank card details. The 3-D Secure protocol is used for additional cardholder authentication. If your issuing Bank supports this technology, you will be redirected to its server for additional identification. For details on the rules and methods of additional identification, contact the Bank that issued your card.

The online payment service is provided in accordance with the rules of the Visa, MasterCard and UnionPay international payment systems, on the principles of confidentiality and payment security; the most up-to-date methods of verification, encryption and data transfer over closed communication channels are used for this. Bank card details are entered in a secure window on the payment page of ForteBank JSC.

In the fields on the payment page you need to enter the card number, the cardholder's name, the card expiry date and the three-digit security code (CVV2 for VISA or CVC2 for MasterCard). All the required details are shown on the surface of the bank card.

CVV2 / CVC2 is the three-digit security code on the back of the card.

Next, in the same window, your issuing bank's page will open for you to enter the 3-D Secure code. If you do not have a static 3-D Secure set up, the code will be sent to your phone number by SMS. If the 3-D Secure code does not reach you, contact your issuing bank.

3-D Secure is the most advanced technology for securing card payments on the internet. It makes it possible to reliably confirm the identity of the cardholder performing the transaction and to minimise the risk of fraudulent card operations.

Security guarantees

ForteBank JSC protects and processes your bank card data in accordance with the PCI DSS 3.0 security standard. Information is transferred to the payment gateway using SSL encryption. Further transfer of information takes place over closed banking networks with the highest level of reliability. ForteBank JSC does not pass your card data to us or to any other third parties. The 3-D Secure protocol is used for additional cardholder authentication.

If you have any questions about a payment you have made, you can contact the payment service customer support by email at spa@imbir.kz.

Security of online payments

The personal information you provide (name, address, phone number, e-mail, credit card number) is confidential and is not disclosed. Your credit card details are transmitted only in encrypted form and are not stored on our web server.

The security of internet payment processing is guaranteed by ForteBank JSC. All operations with payment cards are carried out in accordance with the requirements of VISA International, MasterCard, UnionPay and other payment systems. Specialised security technologies for online card payments are used during data transfer, and data is processed on the secure high-technology server of the processing company.

Paying by payment card is safe, because:

  • The authorisation system guarantees to the buyer that the details of their payment card (number, expiry date, CVV2/CVC2) will not fall into the hands of fraudsters, since this data is not stored on the authorisation server and cannot be stolen.
  • The buyer enters their payment details directly in the authorisation system of ForteBank JSC rather than on the online shop's website, so the buyer's card payment details are not available to third parties.
